# weeklify-showcase | Reach your goals in 12 weeks and not in 12 months.
This is a showcase of my upcoming app (it will be uploaded to the AppStore after the testing phase). 
It is based on the method from the book "The 12 Week Year".

## Overview
Plan your goals in 12-week cycles. Create a plan with all the tasks you need to do, so you actually reach it. The theory is if you do 85% of the tasks every week you will eventually reach the Goal.

## Features

- Create multiple 12 Week cycles
- Create different types of Tasks
  - x times a week
  - List
  - Counter
  - Budget
- A lot of different widgets in all kind of sizes
- View all of the 12 weeks and their tasks at once
- Live activities
  - Timer for counter task
  - Shows deadlines due this week
  - Shows flagged task in the current week
- Deadlines
  - Always on top of the week Views, so you never miss one
- Calendar integration
  - Shows deadlines in your calendar
  - Create tasks from calendar events
- Reflection
  - Rate your past week
  - What went well and what did not
  - Describe the problems that stopped you from fulfilling a task
  - Find and save a solution in case that problem occurs again
